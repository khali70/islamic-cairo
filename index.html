About the walk

Five stops, two kilometres

Stop 01

الغورية

Al-Ghouriya

Begin between Sultan al-Ghuri's mosque and dome (1505), with the market running beneath. Return after dark for the Tanoura.

Stop 02

الجامع الأزهر

Al-Azhar Mosque

Founded by the Fatimids in 970 and still a place of study. Cross the white marble courtyard and count its five minarets.

Stop 03

الحسين

Al-Hussein

The square before Sayyidna al-Hussein Mosque fills at sunset. Take mint tea at El Fishawy, pouring for over two centuries.

Stop 04

سوق الصاغة

Souq al-Sagha

The goldsmiths' lane beside Khan el-Khalili. Watch names cut into gold, haggle gently, then follow the lantern light.

Stop 05

شارع المعز

Al-Muizz Street

The Fatimid high street, from Bab Zuweila to Bab al-Futuh: one kilometre of the finest medieval monuments in Cairo.

Good to know

  • Tanoura nights

    Whirling at the Ghuri complex. Arrive early.

  • Tea at Fishawy

    Mint tea or sahlab, any hour.

  • Walk after dark

    Lit domes, cooler air, calmer lanes.

  • Mosque manners

    Cover shoulders and knees, shoes off inside.
